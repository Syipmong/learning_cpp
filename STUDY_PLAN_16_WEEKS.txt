C++ 16-WEEK STUDY PLAN
======================

Study 5 days per week, 45-90 minutes per day. Use the daily template after
each session. Spend the sixth day reviewing and the seventh day resting.

WEEK 01: Setup, compilation, output, variables, arithmetic
WEEK 02: Input, conditions, loops, validation
WEEK 03: Functions, scope, recursion, fundamentals exercises
WEEK 04: Arrays, strings, vectors, references, pointers
WEEK 05: Structs, enums, exceptions, file streams
WEEK 06: Core exercise review and contact-list mini-project
WEEK 07: Classes, constructors, invariants, const member functions
WEEK 08: RAII, composition, inheritance, virtual functions
WEEK 09: STL containers and iterators
WEEK 10: Algorithms, lambdas, ranges, text-search mini-project
WEEK 11: optional, variant, smart pointers, move semantics
WEEK 12: Threads, mutexes, jthread, futures
WEEK 13: Function/class templates and type deduction
WEEK 14: Concepts, constexpr, generic data structures
WEEK 15: CMake libraries, testing, warnings, sanitizers, benchmarks
WEEK 16: Complete one capstone and write its technical documentation

WEEKLY REVIEW QUESTIONS
-----------------------

1. What can I explain without looking at notes?
2. Which compiler error taught me something?
3. What edge case did I test?
4. What ownership and lifetime rules does my code use?
5. What will I implement next week?

Do not rush to the next week if you cannot solve the previous week's exercises.
