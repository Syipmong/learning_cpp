STL MINI-PROJECTS
=================

01_TEXT_SEARCH
--------------
Search files recursively, count matches, rank files, and report unreadable
paths without stopping the whole search.

02_LOG_ANALYZER
---------------
Parse log lines into records, group by severity, and report the busiest hour.

03_CSV_REPORT
-------------
Read a CSV-like file, validate rows, sort records, and generate a summary.
Document the limitations of your parser.
